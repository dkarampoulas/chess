Analytical Insight on Match Won Probabilities \(P( ext{Outcome} \mid T = t)\):

1. Early Phase (\(t < 6 ext{ min}\)): Matches terminating early are driven by opening blunders and tactical traps (e.g. Scholar's mate variants, trapped queens). White captures the first-move advantage initiative, achieving win rates over 55%.

2. Middlegame Phase (\(6 \le t \le 14 ext{ min}\)): Strategic equilibrium. Black's defensive resources equalize, and outcomes converge toward standard master distributions (\(pprox 48\%\) White, \(45\%\) Black).

3. Endgame & Time Scramble (\(t > 15 ext{ min}\)): Clock attrition. As pieces leave the board into simplified King-and-Pawn endgames, the Draw probability \(P( ext{Draw} \mid t)\) rises steeply by 2.5× to 3× compared to middlegames.
